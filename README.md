# Lumenveil

**Volumetric fog, night lights, sun and moon shafts, and living water for the World of Warcraft 3.3.5a client.**

Lumenveil is a free, open-source graphics mod for the Windows client of World of Warcraft, version 3.3.5a (build
12340). You copy a few files next to `Wow.exe`, and the game you already have gets drifting fog that settles in
valleys, light shafts through trees, lamps and torches that light the world around them at night, and water that moves
with the wind.

It draws on top of the game's own picture. It contains no game files, textures or models, and it changes none of the
game's files.

> Press **Ctrl+F12** in the game to switch Lumenveil off and on and see the difference for yourself.

## Before and after

Each pair is the same moment with Lumenveil switched off (left) and on (right), using its compare key
(Ctrl+F12). Weather, clouds and creatures can move a little between the two shots. The client they were taken
on also runs the [Project Reforged](https://projectreforged.github.io/wotlk/) HD texture and model packs and the ReShade preset described in
[docs/RESHADE.md](docs/RESHADE.md), in both shots of each pair, so the left shot is not quite the game as it ships.

About half of the pairs were taken with a newer development build, which also draws the land beyond the view
distance; that part is not in release 0.1.0.

## What it adds

- **Fog you can walk through.** Layers of haze, ground mist and distance fog, marched per pixel in 3D. Banks of fog lie
  on the ground and pool in valleys and over lakes, drift with the wind, thicken in rain, and lift off the ground at
  dawn. Dry zones get warm dust, green ones a humid haze.
- **Sun and moon shafts.** Light shafts through trees, buildings and mountains, also from a sun or moon outside the
  picture. What the camera has seen is remembered, so the shafts don't flicker at the edge of the screen.
- **Night lights.** Lamps, torches, braziers, candles and lit windows placed in the world light the ground, walls,
  plants and characters around them in their own colour, with shadows, and glow in the fog. Towns stay visible from
  far away at night. Nights are darker than in the plain game, so the lights matter.
- **Spell light.** Spells, enchants and glowing projectiles light their surroundings.
- **Water.** Waves that follow the wind and the weather, reflections that stay put as you turn, sun glitter, rings
  where rain hits the water, ripples around you, and lamps reflected at night.
- **Wet surfaces** (off in the shipped settings; switch on in Ctrl+F7). In rain, stone and soil darken and shine and
  puddles mirror the torches; grass doesn't turn into a mirror.
- **Relief** (off in the shipped settings; switch on in Ctrl+F7). Depth on building walls, floors and models, from
  height maps the mod makes in the game from your own textures.
- **A look for each zone.** 37 zones are tuned by hand for dawn, day, dusk and night, in clear weather, rain, snow and
  sandstorms. Everywhere else uses the global look.
- **A longer view.** The view distance on the old continents is lifted, and the distance fog follows it.
- **In-game settings.** Every effect has a slider in the settings window (**Ctrl+F7**), and changes show at once.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11.
- The 3.3.5a client, build 12340, any language. Lumenveil checks the client's code before it attaches and stays off on
  any other build.
- A graphics card with Direct3D 9 and pixel shader 3.0 support. The effects are heavy: a recent mid-range card is
  recommended, and every effect can be turned down.
- Memory: the 3.3.5a client is a 32-bit program. A very long view distance uses more of its memory; if the game runs
  out, see [docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md#the-game-closes-with-not-enough-memory-resources).

## Install

1. Download the latest `Lumenveil-<version>.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases) page. If your antivirus warns
   about it, see [Antivirus warnings](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md#antivirus-warnings) for how to check the files.
2. Open the zip and drag everything in it into your game folder, the one that holds `Wow.exe`.
3. Double-click **`Lumenveil-Setup.cmd`** once. It reads your own game files and writes where the world's lamps and
   water are (`lampdata.bin`, `liquiddata.bin`). This takes one to five minutes and is only needed once.
4. Start the game as you always do.

To uninstall, double-click `Lumenveil-Uninstall.cmd`, or delete the files listed in [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md).
Details, and using Lumenveil with other graphics mods, are in [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md).

### With ReShade

Lumenveil works with ReShade, which then adds its effects on top of Lumenveil's. The screenshots above were taken
with ReShade on DXVK and a preset of SMAA anti-aliasing and FakeHDR, with ambient occlusion and a light grade on
Ctrl+F10. [docs/RESHADE.md](docs/RESHADE.md) describes that setup, includes the preset, and explains how to install
ReShade with Lumenveil.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl+F12** | Switches Lumenveil off and on, to compare with the plain game |
| **Ctrl+F7** | Opens the settings window |
| Ctrl+F3 | Opens the region panel, where you can tune the look of each zone |
| Ctrl+F4 | Switches relief on and off |
| Ctrl+F6 | Switches the belt lantern on and off |
| Ctrl+F5 | Saves your place, time, weather and camera to a file |

Every key can be changed in `CoAVolFog.ini`. The settings are explained in [docs/SETTINGS.md](docs/SETTINGS.md).

## Before you play online

Lumenveil only changes what your own screen shows. It sends nothing over the network, reads no account data, plays
nothing for you and shows nothing the game hides, and it changes none of the game's files. To draw its effects it does
attach to the running game: it wraps the game's Direct3D device and redirects a few of the client's drawing calls in
memory.

**Any change to the game client may be against the terms that apply to you or the rules of the service you connect
to, and anti-cheat software may detect it. Read those terms first.** Lumenveil doesn't hide from or get around any
anti-cheat or protection. It comes with no warranty, and you use it at your own risk.

## What's not included, and why

Lumenveil is code only. It contains no files from the game, no part of its program code, and nothing converted from
them: no textures, models, maps or game tables. The data it needs about the world (where the lamps and water are) is
made on your own computer from your own copy of the game by `Lumenveil-Setup.cmd`, and never leaves it. It doesn't
include the game and doesn't say where to get it. See [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md).

Please don't attach game files, or data made from them, to issues or pull requests.
